# The Mapmaker

## Visual direction

A playful illustrated comic in expressive black ink with teal and gold color on cream paper. The fictional maker wears a teal work jacket and carries a gold pencil. Keep that design consistent. Exactly four panels in a 2x2 grid, read left to right and then top to bottom. Rectangular narration boxes hold the quoted captions; white speech balloons hold the quoted dialogue. Art instructions and section headings are not lettering. The ordinary workshop and new group are fictional settings.

## Panel 1 — The familiar workshop

Art: Wide view of a busy workshop. The maker and neighbors draw diagrams linking notes, sketches and ideas on a large shared wall.

Caption: "In the maker's workshop, everyone knew how to turn a tangle of ideas into a map."

## Panel 2 — A new room

Art: The maker enters another room where a group has filled a table with disconnected notes and sketches. Everyone looks puzzled rather than helpless.

Caption: "In the next town, the group had plenty of ideas. They could not yet see how the pieces fit."

Dialogue: Group member: "Where do we start?"

## Panel 3 — A useful power

Art: The maker draws one gold line across the table. Lines of light link the notes into a clear map, while group members move their own pieces into place.

Caption: "The maker could spot the connections and help everyone find a path through the work."

Dialogue: Maker: "Let's put these two together."

## Panel 4 — Room for another question

Art: The group builds from the shared map. The maker notices another enormous stack of notes arriving on a small cart and gives a wry smile.

Caption: "The map gave them a place to begin."

Dialogue: Group member: "We have a few more ideas."

Dialogue: Maker: "We'll need a bigger wall."

## Editorial notes

This example is wholly fictional. It demonstrates a four-panel script, explicit scene-setting narration and a particular ability. No text-generation model or account access is needed to initialize it. This note must never be sent as image lettering.
