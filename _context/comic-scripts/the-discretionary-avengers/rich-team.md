# The Discretionary Avengers

## Visual direction

Eight-panel absurdly wealthy superhero-team parody. Glossy billionaire-tech armor, luxury-resort excess, ostentatious cavern headquarters, immaculate influencer lighting; sincere comic-book spectacle supporting a relentlessly silly account of money as power. This is an explicitly fictional alternate universe, not a claim about anyone's wealth, salary, family or private life. Four recognizable heroes from supplied sheets, fresh rich costumes as directed by parent. Marlon bald with gray beard in white/gold/black billionaire tech armor, no helmet; Jonah glasses in navy pinstripe tech suit, dual gold cards/lanyards and watches; Jordan long dark hair in teal silk equestrian resort couture with black riding boots and gold jewelry; Madeleine strawberry-blonde bangs, black/emerald botanical silk luxury with gold accessories and silver pendant. All faces visible, no masks. Big cream RECTANGULAR narration boxes, short clear balloons; exact text below only. Four panels per page, 2x2. No earnest ending, no moral lecture, no real brand logos required. Each panel under 35 reader-facing words.

## Panel 1 — The origin of power

Art: Magnificent billionaire cave. Marlon steps from a black helicopter directly onto a velvet moving walkway that carries him three feet to his desk. No crime or bereavement origin montage; enormous wallet-shaped reactor glows behind him.

Caption: “In this entirely fictional universe, Marlon's origin story was very short.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “I had money.”

## Panel 2 — The double life

Art: Jonah reveals two glittering watches, two phones and two matching private helicopters on opposite landing pads. His two lavish office doors sit side-by-side. A second golden bank vault unfurls theatrically behind the first.

Caption: “Jonah had two jobs. Naturally, he had twice as much money.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “I maintain a residence in both payrolls.”

## Panel 3 — The island was included

Art: Jordan at breathtaking equestrian island resort, artful tropical white-lotus-style luxury, lounging on a horse-shaped chaise while her actual pampered horse drinks from a gold-rimmed bucket. Hovering phones capture her arrival; staff carry surfboards and huge canvas art.

Caption: “Jordan's formative struggle was choosing between the riding estate and the island.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “Then I discovered islands can have horses.”

## Panel 4 — The private collection

Art: Madeleine reclines in an enormous botanical conservatory spa/library as velvet-gloved attendants deliver rare plants and illuminated grimoires on silver trays. A jeweled scanner labels the endless personal collection. Ring light doubles as an occult halo.

Caption: “Madeleine collected botanical wisdom. Then the botanicals. Then the country house containing them.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “The servants are alphabetized.”

## Panel 5 — A common purpose

Art: Video wall in Marlon's cave summons the other three from increasingly ludicrous luxury locations. Dramatic city skyline beyond. Marlon stands at head of huge polished strategy table, straight-faced urgency.

Caption: “At last, Marlon summoned them for a mission no ordinary team could afford.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “We need a larger room for the equipment.”

## Panel 6 — The upgrade

Art: Colossal cutaway cave renovation. Jonah orders two command centers; Jordan rides a horse down a marble vehicle ramp beside surf pool; Madeleine uses expensive holographic drones to levitate purchase orders over greenhouse wing. Marlon compares tiny versus colossal armor prototype.

Caption: “Jonah ordered two command centers. Jordan added a stable. Madeleine requested a climate-controlled forbidden wing.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “Does the armor come in institutional pricing?”

## Panel 7 — The first emergency

Art: Red alarm erupts. Four armored heroes swivel from their absurdly extravagant consoles. A small laptop center stage displays a humble free-plan limit icon without text; their shocked faces receive cinematic red light. Marlon grips giant black corporate card.

Caption: “Then came the emergency they had trained their entire lives to face.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “We've reached the free-plan limit.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “Stand back.”

## Panel 8 — Unaccountable power

Art: Full grand group shot of all four rich heroes in magnificent bespoke gear on cave balcony, immense newly purchased machines behind, horses and rare trees framed by absurd theatrical fog. Marlon proudly raises corporate card; others pose like a ludicrous luxury campaign.

Caption: “The upgrade was complete. The world would never be under-equipped again.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “Who authorized the fog machine?”

Dialogue: Marlon: “Nobody. We're vigilantes.”

## Editorial notes

Fictional rich-team parody explicitly commissioned by the user. The two-jobs/twice-the-money premise is a joke, not a salary statement. Resort wealth, servants, estates, spending and personality exaggerations are invented, not biographical claims. All four are satirized; no trauma needed to explain any fortune. Motif escalation: wealth → multiplied personal luxury → institutional architecture → trivial paywall as supervillain → vigilante fog purchase. Dialogue speaker names are not lettering.
