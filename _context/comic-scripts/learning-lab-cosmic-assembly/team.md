## Visual direction

Bombastic 1960s Jack Kirby/Fantastic Four-era superhero adventure: massive blocky geometry, heavy brush blacks, bold foreshortening, red/yellow/cyan primaries, Ben-Day dots and black cosmic crackle. All four distinct identities/costumes match supplied CLASSIC sheets. Marlon bald gray beard, black/silver suit/cape, red pencil. Jordan dark long hair, teal suit with coral chevron/gold belt and bracers. Madeleine strawberry-blonde bangs, GREEN/YELLOW jumpsuit with fern emblem, white belt/boots, NO purple cloak. Jonah wavy brown-gray hair, round black glasses, cobalt/saffron suit, white bracket badge, NO coat. Each person has powers ACQUIRED through a separate extraordinary exposure, not born as mutants. This is an alternate fictional adventure, no actual accident asserted. Four panels per portrait page in a clear 2×2 grid, pale-yellow rectangular narration and sparse white dialogue, every quoted text verbatim. Final panel exactly four heroes together.
