# The Institute for Your Own Good

## Visual direction

An entirely fictional 1980s corporate-gothic supervillain alternate universe. Dense expressive black inks, acid-magenta and sickly-green spot colors, cold cobalt shadows, coarse printed halftones, dramatic angular architecture, absurdly expensive sinister tailoring. A cathedral-sized private institute combines black marble boardrooms, occult botanical archives, a red editorial machine, and institutional fluorescent corridors. Match the NEW villain-edition ensemble reference, not the modern resort costumes. Four panels per page in an exact 2x2 grid, crisp cream rectangular narration and white balloons; no captions outside panels and no extra text. Keep faces recognizable: Marlon bald gray beard; Jonah wavy brown hair and glasses; Madeleine strawberry-blonde hair with bangs and silver pendant; Jordan long dark hair. Each person has an independent dangerous power. This is no claim about actual beliefs or conduct.

## Panel 1 — A generous offer

Art: Monumental black-marble private institute at night, enormous four-part stained-glass window with a pencil, two facing profiles, a botanical index, and a color wheel. Four villains stand equally prominent on the entrance balcony. Anonymous visitors enter below through doors shaped like an open book.

Caption: “In another fictional universe, four gifted people founded an institute to save the world from uncertainty.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “The brochure says participation is voluntary.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “For now.”

## Panel 2 — Marlon, The Final Draft

Art: Bald gray-bearded Marlon in black architectural cape with crimson lining operates a colossal red-pencil machine. Behind him, tangled luminous paths of thought straighten into a single narrow red corridor. His face combines genuine concentration and absolute confidence.

Caption: “Marlon could find a pattern in any confusion. As THE FINAL DRAFT, he erased every pattern except his own.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “Your life has an unnecessary subplot.”

## Panel 3 — Jonah, The Double Bind

Art: Bespectacled Jonah in immaculate two-tone navy and ivory pinstripe suit stands at the junction of two grand passageways. His two gold hands manipulate ribbon-like legal clauses: both doorways visibly curve into the same cage. No incidental legal text.

Caption: “Jonah understood how arguments worked across worlds. As THE DOUBLE BIND, he made every disagreement arrive at consent.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “You may choose either entrance.”

## Panel 4 — Madeleine, The Closed Index

Art: Madeleine in emerald-black botanical robes with angular shoulders and silver pendant holds a gilded grimoire. Living plants, small storm clouds and luminous human silhouettes hang suspended inside vast catalog drawers. A vine that reaches outside a drawer becomes a neat diagram.

Caption: “Madeleine made meaningful connections between stories and living things. As THE CLOSED INDEX, she permitted nothing to exist without a category.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “We have no field for that feeling.”

## Panel 5 — Jordan, The Chromarch

Art: Jordan on an ominous white horse wears sweeping violet-teal equestrian cape and angular gold collar. Her prismatic scepter floods a drab square with astonishing color; every visitor is forced into the same jewel-toned outfit and identical elegant pose. Eye contact shows delighted conviction.

Caption: “Jordan brought color and possibility to sterile places. As THE CHROMARCH, she made her taste a law of nature.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “You are free to express my vision.”

## Panel 6 — The perfected city

Art: Huge bird's-eye view through four tall institute windows. The four villains look down together at a beautiful geometric city. Every street arrow points inward, every garden has matching numbered shapes without actual numerals, every person moves in the same direction in beautiful coordinated clothes.

Caption: “Together, they built a city with no loose ends, unresolved arguments, unclassified experiences, or unfortunate curtains.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “Nothing falls through the gaps.”

## Panel 7 — The margin

Art: Close-up of a small anonymous visitor's hand writing WHY? in a brochure margin with a plain pencil. The little question becomes a jagged white crack through the perfect printed page. Four villains visible blurred in background, suddenly alarmed. Only the specified two words appear on the brochure and in dialogue.

Caption: “Then someone wrote in the margin.”

Lettering: “WHY?”

Dialogue: Marlon: “Who left room for that?”

## Panel 8 — Expansion

Art: Four villains at equal positions around an enormous black-marble table, collaboratively unrolling a blueprint of an even more imposing institute wing shaped like a question mark locked in a cage. Behind them builders already wall up the library margins with towering blank stone slabs. Jordan's horse peers through the boardroom door.

Caption: “By morning, they had established the Department of Further Questions.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “All questions welcome.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “Answers by permission only.”

This eight-panel ensemble introduces four villain identities; it is separate from the four modern affluent antihero origins. All malicious conduct and supernatural abilities are fictional. Source strengths remain identifiable. The user was offered a scope choice asynchronously; absent a response, one ensemble is the stated default.

Art review: The selected first page places the opening brochure line with Marlon and the reply with Madeleine.
