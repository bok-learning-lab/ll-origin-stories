# Learning Lab: Unclassified

## Visual direction

Very dark gritty 1990s-and-later mutant ensemble graphic novel. Oppressive black architecture, rain, underground spaces, hard angular shadows, scratchy crosshatching and deep chiaroscuro. Desaturated charcoal and bone; Marlon RED lines, Jordan forbidden chromatic streaks, Madeleine SILVER-GREEN thorn structures, Jonah ACID-LIME routes. All four identities and DARK costumes exactly match sheets. Marlon bald gray beard in battered black longcoat/half-cape. Jordan dark long hair, black riding jacket/trousers, subtle teal/coral seams, gold cuffs. Madeleine strawberry-blonde bangs/braidedcrown, black angular coat with OXBLOOD lining, silverpendant, heavy boots, NO purplecloak. Jonah round black glasses, wavy brown-grayhair, charcoal hoodless longcoat with raggedhem and acidlimeinnerseam. These are INNATE mutations, never radiation-created. Fictional authoritarian city allegory, not accusations about Harvard or actual lived trauma. Four strong panels per portrait page, 2×2 grid, readable bone rectangular narration boxes, minimal speech, exact quoted words. Keep grim stakes and unresolved danger, no warm friendly campus makeover. Final panel shows allfour equal heroes in a defiant nocturnal teamshot.

## Panel 1 — Classified

Art: A black rain-lashed city of immense filing-cabinet-like towers. Anonymous minds reduced to rigid glowing grid silhouettes by an overhead classification machine. In an abandoned lower workshop Marlon reads impossible red connections beneath the official grid. He looks tired and dangerous, not smiling.

Caption: “The city had a category for every mind. Difference was filed as a fault.”

Caption: “Below its towers, Marlon began leaving a door open.”

## Panel 2 — The signal below

Art: Marlon in battered black coat draws a small red connected-line mark on an underground door. In the distance three faint distinct responses—color, thornlight, lime geometry—appear in the rain. This is mutual recognition, not a machine giving them powers.

Caption: “They had been born different. Pressure had made the difference visible.”

Dialogue: Marlon: “You don't have to explain it at the door.”

## Panel 3 — Uncontainable color

Art: Jordan in black ridinghero clothes presses a hand against a gray wall. Forbidden teal/coral color bleeds from her fingers through its imposed grid, making an opening. Her face furious, hairwindblown, stance defiant. No cheerful smile. She moves toward refuge carrying power she cannot entirely hide.

Caption: “Jordan's color would not stay inside the lines.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “Good.”

## Panel 4 — The forbidden index

Art: Madeleine in black oxblood-lined coat emerges from a ruined subterranean archive. Silver-green thorn roots and botanical logic carry unclassifiable pages around her; some form a protective black thornlike silhouette against searchlight. Fierce intelligent expression, silverpendant glints.

Caption: “Madeleine could make a living system out of what the city tried to erase.”

## Panel 5 — Between the lines

Art: Jonah blackglasses/charcoalcoat sees lime structuralroutes through a sealed urban corridor. He opens a route between a dim writing classroom and the underground workshop, keeping both doors available. His burden is seeing every constraint; determination makes him act.

Caption: “Jonah could see the walls inside an instruction—and the passage they concealed.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “There is another way through.”

## Panel 6 — The test

Art: The classification engine's enormous brutal geometric head pushes down toward their refuge. ALL FOUR face it together: Marlon reveals red structuraljoints, Jordan tears open spectrum cracks, Madeleine grows a living alternative through its rigid lattice, Jonah opens limeroutes for people behind them. Intense jagged superhero action with abstract machine as enemy; no gore/realinstitutional insignia.

Caption: “When the city came to close the door, it found more than one kind of refusal.”

## Panel 7 — Space to remain different

Art: Inside the underground Lab, the four guard/work at distinct stations while unnamed learners explore unapproved projects. Walls scarred, rainleaks, many shadows, but thought moves freely between divergent paths. Threatening searchlights continue above; no total victory or cheerful cure.

Caption: “They made a place where a mind could remain complicated.”

Caption: “Outside, the searchlights kept moving.”

## Panel 8 — Unclassified

## Editorial notes

Assistant-written alternate mutant-universe frame. The city, classifications, surveillance, machine attack and emotional burden are fictional genre staging, not real events, diagnoses or institutional allegations. All powers are innate latent differences, distinct from acquired exposure origins. Professional histories inform the form of each gift. The ending is a precarious refuge and alliance, not a claim outsiders are cured or a tidy uplifting moral. Existing new mutant sheets reused.
