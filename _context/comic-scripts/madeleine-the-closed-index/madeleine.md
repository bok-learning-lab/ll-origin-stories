# Madeleine: The Closed Index

## Visual direction

1980s corporate-gothic supervillain comic. Hand-drawn expressive ink with dense black masses, coarse printed halftones, aged cream paper, acid magenta, emerald, crimson and cobalt. Angular architecture, gothic research rooms and sinister corporate patronage. LANDSCAPE 3:2 page containing exactly FOUR panels in a 2x2 grid, equal quadrants, thick black borders and cream gutters. Large legible cream rectangular narration boxes and white speech balloons. Match villain ensemble sheet faces and final costumes. No photographic rendering, modern smartphones, brand logos or extra lettering. Speaker labels are instructions, not printed words. Each page must tell a visible story, with varied camera distance and concrete action. The protagonist starts in the plain clothes specified in panels 1–4, with the SAME face and hair as the reference. The full reference villain costume appears when specified, firmly by panels 5–8. Only this protagonist from the ensemble appears. Supporting characters must be distinct anonymous adults. All wrongdoing, foundations, supernatural objects and origins are fictional. No invented real hiring motives or trauma.

## Panel 1 — A living relation

Art: Madeleine with strawberry-blonde bangs and silver pendant in a simple green dress works at an old botanical library. Pressed plants, annotated folktales and a living vine span her desk; she draws meaningful connections between them.

Caption: “Madeleine found connections among folklore, plants, and the systems people used to understand them.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “The same flower means something different in every story.”

## Panel 2 — The threatened archive

Art: A foundation official in gray raincoat watches water rising across a gothic conservatory-library floor. Madeleine carries manuscripts and a potted vine up a stair. Rain bursts through a cracked domed roof. No disaster victims, only endangered collection.

Caption: “In this fictional origin, a flood threatened the collection she had spent years making legible.”

Dialogue: Official: “We can save the rare books. Leave the ordinary plants.”

## Panel 3 — The impossible catalog

Art: Madeleine opens a gold-bound forbidden index above the rising water. A pressed leaf in the book becomes a perfectly living miniature plant in a floating square compartment. Her ordinary dress begins to acquire angular emerald shoulders.

Caption: “She found an index that could preserve whatever she described precisely enough.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “Then I’ll describe the whole garden.”

## Panel 4 — The rescue

Art: Wide magical conservation: Madeleine sweeps a gilded stylus through the air, moving books, plants and the entire rainstorm into glowing catalog drawers. Library floor becomes dry. Storm clouds remain visible in a glass-front drawer, still beautifully gray.

Caption: “She catalogued the plants, the books, and finally the storm. Nothing was lost.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “Not one leaf.”

## Panel 5 — The stillness

Art: Days later, close shot of the rescued vine in a glass drawer. Its smallest new leaf presses against the frame but cannot unfold. An anonymous gardener with short black hair and work apron holds an ordinary unconfined shoot for comparison. Madeleine wears full villain robes.

Caption: “Nothing changed, either.”

Dialogue: Gardener: “It’s alive. Why won’t it grow?”

## Panel 6 — The choice of preservation

Art: Madeleine sees the live vine reaching toward the drawer’s open crack. Her hand deliberately shuts the drawer instead of freeing it. Her reflective face hardens. On the neighboring desk, the new shoot casts a branching shadow.

Caption: “She could release the collection. Instead, she made growth an error in the record.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “The description was complete yesterday.”

## Panel 7 — Everything in its place

Art: THE CLOSED INDEX towers in immense archive with gilded grimoire and silver pendant, suspending forests, clouds, schools of fish and human gatherings in glowing numbered-looking but unlettered drawer compartments. Ladders and mechanical rails span a city-sized system.

Caption: “As THE CLOSED INDEX, she archived the world before it could become something else.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “Change is how things go missing.”

## Panel 8 — A new accession

Art: At her beautiful archive desk Madeleine studies a living root that has cracked the seam between two supposedly sealed drawers. Rather than remove it, she calmly lowers an even larger glass case over both drawers and the root. Her smile is satisfied.

Caption: “A root grew between the categories. She expanded the catalog.”

Dialogue: Madeleine: “There. Now even the exception belongs to me.”

## Editorial notes

Agent-authored exact script, written before generation. Biographical skills come from the current project brief; patrons, crises, artifacts, choices, actions, dialogue and malicious traits are alternate-universe inventions. The eight-panel structure establishes a useful skill, grants a literal power, shows a beneficial first use, introduces an independent person or living process, then depicts the protagonist deliberately suppressing it. The final image preserves an unresolved flaw and a dry comic beat. Supporting characters are anonymous adults, not other workshop participants.
