# Jordan: The Taste Regime

## Visual direction

Contemporary luxury-satire superhero comic. Exquisite expressive ink illustration, rich cinematic color, sharply observed social comedy, extravagant Mediterranean island resort architecture, ominously perfect sunset, cream rectangular narration boxes and crisp white speech balloons. Exactly four panels per page in a 2x2 grid, generous gutters, clear left-to-right then downward reading. Faces and costume identities match the supplied existing affluent ensemble sheet. Dynamic varied compositions, not four talking heads. No real company logos. Only exact specified lettering; speaker names are directions, not printed text. This is an explicitly fictional affluent antihero alternate universe. Wealth, businesses, behavior, resorts and technology are inventions, not biographical claims. Preserve the protagonist’s identity from the ensemble reference; do not add the other three protagonists unless requested. No masks.

## Panel 1 — The home world

Art: Grand view of a mythical artist island: sea, green mountains, farms, colorful studios and people riding horses. Jordan with long dark hair rides a white horse, teal silk blouse, white riding trousers and black boots.

Caption: “Jordan came from a mythical island of artists: sea and mountains like Hawaii, farms and horses like rural Georgia.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “Even the stables had opinions.”

## Panel 2 — The fortune

Art: Jordan in teal equestrian resort couture at bright outdoor design table. Rich anonymous patrons compete with black cards for her vivid paint swatches; gold drones and textile samples on table.

Caption: “Her arts-and-education instincts made possibilities visible. In this fictional universe, luxury clients paid absurdly well for them.”

Dialogue: Client: “Could we purchase your spontaneity?”

## Panel 3 — The insult

Art: Jordan rides her horse into an appallingly beige resort courtyard. A beige-suited developer glances dismissively at her richly colored concept drawing. Her expression is sweet and lethal.

Caption: “Then a developer bought the island’s gathering place and called her alternative fun.”

Dialogue: Developer: “We’re aiming for something more serious.”

## Panel 4 — The equipment

Art: Low-angle heroic reveal: Jordan on white horse with intricate gold saddle-mounted control system; fleet of high-end painting and fabric drones unfolds above marble stable, teal cape billowing.

Caption: “Jordan assembled an arsenal of pigment drones, projection rigs, and extremely well-equipped horses.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “Bless your heart.”

## Panel 5 — The reclamation

Art: Spectacular action: drones spray sweeping cobalt, coral, emerald and gold across beige courtyard while residents paint alongside Jordan. Horse leaps shallow fountain, silk ribbons unfurl. No attacks on people.

Caption: “By noon, the square belonged to color again.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “Open the gates. Everyone gets a brush.”

## Panel 6 — The audience arrives

Art: Same glorious colorful square now packed with influencer phones on sticks and gold ring lights. Jordan poses on horse atop broad steps, surrounded by appreciative artists and cameras.

Caption: “By three, everyone wanted to be seen there. Jordan noticed a problem.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “That jacket is fighting the fountain.”

## Panel 7 — The correction

Art: Jordan at velvet-rope entrance graciously holds up approved fabric swatches. A formerly participating artist in ordinary beige work jacket pauses with paintbrush in hand; gold drones hang like quiet bouncers.

Caption: “The courtyard remained open to everyone who met the palette.”

Dialogue: Artist: “You said everyone gets a brush.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “And a complimentary styling consultation.”

## Panel 8 — The exception

Art: Jordan reclines on vivid tiled resort terrace in absurdly expensive plain beige cashmere, white horse nearby wearing huge teal bow. Below, everyone else wears coordinated vivid colors. Her phone frames a self-portrait.

Caption: “At last, the island expressed complete creative freedom.”

Dialogue: Assistant: “You’re wearing beige.”

Dialogue: Jordan: “On me, it’s subversive.”
