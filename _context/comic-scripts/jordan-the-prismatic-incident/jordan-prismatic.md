# Jordan: The Prismatic Incident

## Visual direction

A glorious 1960s Jack Kirby/Fantastic Four era superhero comic: monumental angular compositions, blocky carved faces, thick confident black brush outlines, dramatic foreshortened hands, chunky machinery, explosive black-dot cosmic energy, limited saturated four-color printing, visible Ben-Day dots, off-white newsprint. NOT photorealism, no smooth rendered 3D faces, no contemporary digital gradient aesthetic. Jordan's likeness remains recognizable in the stylization: long dark-brown hair, strong long oval face/nose, arched eyebrows and amused wry smile. Ancient island clothing is an ivory tunic, teal riding trousers and dark boots; after exposure she gains a bold teal superhero jumpsuit with coral chevron, gold bracers/belt and black riding boots as shown in the new style-specific sheet. Exactly 4 panels in 2x2 grid per page. Large cream rectangular exposition boxes and white speech balloons, robust black comic lettering. Letter only quoted captions/dialogue, no titles or panel numbers. Mythic island ancient stone, horses and cultivated farmland; its sea and mountains resemble Hawaii, its farms/horses rural Georgia as comparisons to our world. All inhabitants artists. No literal modern campus on the home island. The accident creates the power; before it Jordan uses ordinary artistic materials. Every event in this variant is deliberately superhero fiction.

## Panel 1 — Before the impossible

Art: Establishing view of ancient artist island, emerald peaks over blue ocean, terraced farms, stone courtyards, Jordan on bay horse with mounted artists painting murals. Monumental 1960s graphic shapes and rich primary palette; she holds ordinary brushes.

Caption: "Jordan lived on an ancient island where everyone was an artist."

Caption: "Picture Hawaii's mountains and sea, with farmland and horses like rural Georgia."

## Panel 2 — A craft, not yet a power

Art: Jordan shows mounted young adult artists how colored paper and paint make an idea tangible in an open stone workshop. Behind them a huge strange experimental prism apparatus is under construction, geometric brass rings and crystalline optics. Nobody uses magic yet; horse ears and paint jars establish daily world.

Caption: "She used art to help people learn by making things."

Caption: "Color was her craft. It was not yet her power."

## Panel 3 — The experiment

Art: Nightstorm over ancient light tower; huge Kirby machine and suspended prism in stone laboratory. Jordan at manual controls, lightning forks beyond arched windows, frantic angles. One drawable instant just before strike, danger but no injury.

Caption: "One night, Jordan tested a prism built to cast every color at once."

Caption: "Then lightning struck the tower."

## Panel 4 — Exposure

Art: An immense lightning-fed prismatic burst surrounds Jordan's silhouette, angular rainbow shards and heavy black cosmic energy dots, shattering optics, foreshortened outstretched hand. She is engulfed without gore, burns or disfigurement. This is the creation of supernatural chromatic power.

Caption: "The prism shattered. Its impossible light passed straight through her."

Caption: "The accident changed what color could do."

## Panel 5 — No paint required

Art: Morning after. Jordan in new teal/coral/gold hero suit touches a blank stone wall with bare fingers; a tremendous cascade of colored horses and living shapes springs out. She looks at her empty other hand, surprised and delighted. No brushes or paint.

Caption: "After the accident, Jordan no longer needed paint."

Dialogue: Jordan: "That wasn't supposed to happen."

## Panel 6 — Another world's palette

Art: Bright Jordan in hero suit at monochrome Harvard seminar table displaying a small brilliant paper horse to a stiff gray academic. Mid-century graphic austerity, Jordan raised eyebrow. Gray academic alone speaks; clear tail.

Caption: "She brought her new power to Harvard's Faculty of Arts and Sciences."

Dialogue: Gray academic: "Oh, that's fun."

Caption: "Apparently, that was not a compliment."

## Panel 7 — An invitation in color

Art: Jordan throws an enormous arc of living color through the gray seminar room. It becomes colorful materials and forms at a shared worktable, where participants actively construct things themselves. Cosmic Kirby energy and joyful making, not combat.

Caption: "She turned a colorless room into a place people could make things together."

Dialogue: Participant: "Can we try it?"

## Panel 8 — A suitable laboratory

Art: Fully colored Learning Lab creative studio, Jordan's teal/coral/gold suit consistent. A colleague on left admires a brilliantly colored miniature horse conjured on the table; Jordan on right has wry smile. Art supplies, cameras and lights around; no named other staff or generic copyrighted heroes. Two speakers distinct and tails correct.

Caption: "At the Learning Lab, inside the Bok Center, color was a working material."

Dialogue: Colleague: "Oh, that's fun!"

Dialogue: Jordan: "You should see the horses."

## Editorial notes

Agent-authored script, written before image generation. This is a new 1960s exposure/accident origin, not a claim that Jordan experienced any real accident. The prism and lightning CREATE the power; before the event she has arts/education craft but no supernatural color. Retains user-corrected Georgia origin and mythic ancient island explained via comparisons to our world. HGSE arts education informs her methods but is not recast as a literal ancient campus. FAS is the gray-world destination; Learning Lab is inside Bok. Fictional mechanism, costume, events and quotations are documented as fiction. No previous comic assets are replaced.
