# Jordan: In Living Color

## Visual direction

Dramatic idealized graphic superhero comic, bold expressive black ink, controlled halftones, vivid flat color, elegant readable faces. Retain Jordan's likeness from the character sheet: long dark-brown hair, expressive arched brows, long oval face, strong nose, warm wry smile. On her ancient home island she wears an ivory linen riding tunic with dark riding trousers; her modern civilian look is an ivory blouse with dark trousers; heroic costume is a practical teal, coral, and gold riding suit with trousers and boots, no sexualization. Hair stays long and dark; heroic poses and simplified graphic shadows. Exactly four equal panels per page in a 2x2 grid, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom. Cream rectangular narration boxes with black uppercase lettering; dialogue in white rounded speech bubbles. Text specified below must be lettered verbatim; do not letter Art directions or section headings. No additional text. Set aside generous readable box space. The home island is ancient, magical, lush and extravagantly colorful, with weathered stone courtyards, glowing pigments, orchards, tilled fields, horses and dramatic mountains beside turquoise seas; never a modern campus; FAS starts nearly monochrome; the Lab is saturated and welcoming. Every island inhabitant is on horseback, including artists working beside easels. Avoid stereotyped island cultural clothing. This is a fictional arts-and-horses fantasy, not a representation of Hawaiian culture. Consistent bay horse when Jordan rides.

## Panel 1 — A place where color belongs

Art: Grand establishing view of an ancient magical emerald mountain island with turquoise surf, terraced farmland, orchards, grazing horses, weathered stone towers and open stone courtyards bright with art. Jordan rides a bay horse foreground in an ivory linen riding tunic and dark trousers, long hair moving in the breeze. All inhabitants are artists on horses, painting murals or sharing glowing pigments. Luminous painted motifs float gently above the old stone. No modern school, logos, buildings or campus. Farmland and horses recall rural Georgia; seas and mountains recall Hawaii, as comparisons to our world rather than literal geography.

Caption: "Jordan came from an ancient island where everyone was an artist."

Caption: "The fields were fertile, the courtyards full of color—and everyone rode horses."

## Panel 2 — The home world's ordinary magic

Art: Medium shot Jordan on her horse painting a brilliant mural on an ancient stone courtyard wall beside a mounted artist friend. Beyond them: rolling farmland with horses, fertile green terraces, mountains and bright turquoise crashing surf. Jordan casually releases a ribbon of living magical color from her paintbrush; her friend is unsurprised. Ivory linen tunic and dark riding trousers match panel one. Magic is ordinary here. No modern surfboard logos or campus.

Caption: "Imagine the seas and mountains of Hawaii, with farmland and horses like rural Georgia."

Caption: "Here, making things colorful was simply part of making things."

## Panel 4 — A curious local expression

Art: At a drab seminar table Jordan offers the bright handmade paper model. An anonymous gray academic wearing a gray suit studies it with a tight patronizing smile. Jordan raises one eyebrow, amused rather than crushed. Color belongs only to her model and a tiny teal accessory.

Caption: "She soon learned that enthusiasm had a local dialect."

Dialogue: Gray academic: "Oh, that's fun."

Caption: "It did not sound like a compliment."

## Panel 5 — The power made visible

Art: Dramatic low angle Jordan now in the consistent teal, coral, and gold heroic riding suit. In one broad gesture she sweeps a ribbon of living color across the bland seminar room. It turns into murals, paper shapes and bright tools people can use. No destruction, no enemies knocked down. Heroic expression wry and determined.

Caption: "Jordan's power was adding color—and making room for people to create."

Dialogue: Jordan: "Let's try it."

## Panel 6 — Participation

Art: The same seminar table transformed into a busy shared making session. Students build paper structures and draw vivid marks; gray sleeves gradually become colored as participants reach for materials. Jordan helps a participant assemble a model, practical hands-on moment, not magically controlling people.

Caption: "Color spread through the room as people picked up materials and joined in."

Dialogue: Participant: "Can we make another one?"

## Panel 7 — A room already in color

Art: Jordan stands in open double doors looking into a bright creative studio, wearing the same superhero riding suit. In the room are worktables, colored stage lights, art supplies, cameras, sketches, and cheerful unnamed collaborators, all already in full color. Wide welcoming composition. No portraits of other named staff; no invented extra hero identities.

Caption: "At the Learning Lab, inside the Bok Center, Jordan found a room already in full color."

Dialogue: Lab colleague: "What are you making?"

## Panel 8 — Same words, new meaning

Art: Warm humorous final panel. In the Lab Jordan and a colleague admire an elaborate brightly colored tabletop miniature of the island, with tiny horses and a surf wave. Jordan retains same face and costume and an amused raised eyebrow. The colleague beams enthusiastically. Miniature horses echo page one; no solemn inspirational pose.
