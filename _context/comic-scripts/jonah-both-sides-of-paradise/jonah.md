# Jonah: Both Sides of Paradise

## Visual direction

Contemporary luxury-satire superhero comic. Exquisite expressive ink illustration, rich cinematic color, sharply observed social comedy, extravagant Mediterranean island resort architecture, ominously perfect sunset, cream rectangular narration boxes and crisp white speech balloons. Exactly four panels per page in a 2x2 grid, generous gutters, clear left-to-right then downward reading. Faces and costume identities match the supplied existing affluent ensemble sheet. Dynamic varied compositions, not four talking heads. No real company logos. Only exact specified lettering; speaker names are directions, not printed text. This is an explicitly fictional affluent antihero alternate universe. Wealth, businesses, behavior, resorts and technology are inventions, not biographical claims. Preserve the protagonist’s identity from the ensemble reference; do not add the other three protagonists unless requested. No masks.

## Panel 1 — Two worlds

Art: Jonah, wavy brown hair and glasses, navy pinstripe tailored tech suit, two gold watches and two gold passes, stands between modest writing-teaching desks and a fantastically expensive two-ended private launch.

Caption: “Jonah’s real work spans Expos and the Bok Center. His fictional alter ego extended that arrangement to two rival luxury resorts.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “I value continuity.”

## Panel 2 — Reading the room

Art: At a bay-crossing breakfast aboard the launch, Jonah marks two manuscripts with red pencil. Two mirrored resort terraces behind him, identical breakfast platters beside each arm.

Caption: “Writing pedagogy had taught him to hear the argument beneath a complaint.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “When you say community, do you mean nobody else can enter?”

## Panel 3 — The mechanism

Art: Close-up: Jonah calmly holds two engraved gold passes while his paired watches project two private maps. Tailored lapel folds open to expose a tiny expensive communications system. Two marina gates open.

Caption: “He bought paired access systems and became the only person welcome behind both gates.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “My superpower is reciprocal membership.”

## Panel 4 — The disagreement

Art: Huge split composition within one panel: competing resort owners at opposite ends of a marble table, each holding an identical framed empty-bay photograph. Jonah sits between with annotated contract.

Caption: “Each resort promised its guests an exclusive sunset. Unfortunately, they shared a horizon.”

Dialogue: Owner: “Their yacht is in our sunset.”

## Panel 5 — The close reading

Art: Jonah scrutinizes enormous contract pages projected above water. The only highlighted line is the exact quoted phrase in his speech; do not add legal lettering to the projection. Owners lean in.

Caption: “Jonah located the ambiguity everyone else had missed.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “You promised an unobstructed experience. You never specified reality.”

## Panel 6 — The solution

Art: Both terraces now filled with beautifully dressed guests wearing sleek golden augmented-reality sunglasses. In foreground a lens shows clear sea; outside it actual yachts crowd the bay. Jonah demonstrates with two pairs.

Caption: “His glasses removed the rival resort from each guest’s view.”

Dialogue: Guest: “Finally. Somewhere unspoiled.”

## Panel 7 — The inconvenient person

Art: At the dock, an attentive captain points toward a real yacht while a sunglass-wearing owner waves dismissively at the empty-sea illusion. Jonah lifts his own glasses to look at the crowded bay.

Caption: “The captain still needed everyone to acknowledge the boats.”

Dialogue: Captain: “We cannot navigate a marketing promise.”

## Panel 8 — A third position

Art: Jonah enjoys two breakfasts on a lavish floating office between the resorts. He has removed the tinted glasses; a third gold pass rests on an open red-marked manuscript. The clear bay map shows every boat.

Caption: “Jonah made the complete picture available.”

Dialogue: Jonah: “Reality is included in the higher membership tier.”
